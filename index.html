About & Contact

Hi, I’m Sabrina.

I’ve always used photography to document life, capture honest moments, and connect with people. Through taking photos, I get to meet new faces and explore the world.

Originally from China, I lived in Japan for 4 years before moving to Copenhagen, Denmark, where I’ve been based for the last 2 years. Alongside photography, I have a background in spatial design, and I love traveling and journaling my life through comics.

Right now, I’m constantly exploring where these different creative paths meet. Reach out if you’d like to collaborate, create something meaningful, or capture great memories together.
